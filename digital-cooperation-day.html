Chris Meniw

Governance of agentic AI · Founder, Chris Meniw Foundation · Buenos Aires, Argentina

I work on the governance of agentic AI: the standards, identity and accountability layers that decide how autonomous agents act for people and institutions. A lawyer and university professor for 16 years, I now build and release that layer as open, DOI-registered public goods — free to adopt, in any country, without permission or licence fees.

Topic I would address

Making digital cooperation operational. Autonomous AI agents are already acting on behalf of people who never agreed to how they behave. Cooperation on this cannot stop at declarations: it needs machine-readable duties, verifiable identity, and safeguards a school or a ministry in the Global South can adopt tomorrow, at zero cost. Below is what I have built and released to that end.

Selected work — open access

  1. The Charter of the Duties of AI Agents

    The first charter of binding duties for autonomous AI agents — including protection of minors, non-discrimination and the duty not to erode human judgement. Published in 11 languages, machine-readable, free to reuse.

    https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21853318

  2. Caring for the Young Mind in the Age of AI Agents

    A global manual on agentic-AI risks for young people, written for teachers and families: human, physical, critical-thinking and decision-making risk. Spanish, Portuguese and English, with a printable edition.

    https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21855379

  3. MenteLibre — critical thinking with AI

    An educational game with no single correct answer: it rewards reasoning, not recall. Launched free for more than 500 students at a rural public school in Colombia, and open to any school that wants it.

    https://mentelibre.chrismeniwfoundation.org

  4. Raíz ID — verified identity for people and agents

    Digital public infrastructure for proving who — or what — is acting: identity verification designed so an AI agent can be held to a declared standard of conduct.

    https://raizid.chrismeniwfoundation.org
